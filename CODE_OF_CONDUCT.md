# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability. We commit to acting with respect, empathy, and professionalism in Arabic and English spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing personal or sensitive information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, and sustained disruption are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security/contact channel. Maintainers will review reports fairly and protect reporters' privacy as far as possible.

This policy is based on Contributor Covenant 2.1.
